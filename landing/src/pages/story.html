Resolvr started with a manager who made work unbearable, and a folder that should have existed but didn't.

It was rarely one big moment. It was a remark in a meeting, a change to a schedule, an email that left someone out. Each one was easy to explain away. Together they were wearing someone down. When the time came to talk to HR, there were no dates, no notes and no screenshots, only memory, and memory is easy to question.

Ayodapo Ige built Resolvr so that nobody has to start from nothing. It is a private place to write down what happened, when, who was there and how it affected you, with timestamps that show the record was not rewritten later. It keeps your evidence on your own device, encrypted, and helps you see the pattern when you are too close to it.

What we believe

  • Your record belongs to you. It lives on your device, and you decide if and when anyone sees it.
  • Calm is a feature. No alarms, no streaks, no gamification. Just a steady, organised record.
  • Honesty over hype. Resolvr does not promise outcomes. It helps you be clear and prepared.
  • We are not lawyers. Resolvr is not legal advice. When you need advice, we point you to people who can give it.
