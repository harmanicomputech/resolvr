The short version

  • Your incidents, notes and evidence are encrypted and stored on your device. We cannot read them.
  • We only hold your email address if you give it to us (waitlist or account), and encrypted backups if you turn backup on.
  • If you choose to draft a document with AI, only the fields you select are sent, to create that draft.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, no selling or renting data. Ever.

Your record stays on your device

The incidents you log, the people you name, your notes, impact statements and the files in your evidence vault are stored in your browser's storage on your device. Before anything is stored, it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your PIN. The key is kept in memory only while the app is unlocked. We never receive your PIN, your key or the unencrypted contents of your record.

Because we cannot read your record, we cannot recover it if you forget your PIN and have no backup, and we cannot hand its contents to anyone, including your employer.

AI drafting

Drafting a complaint letter or other document is optional and only happens when you ask. Before anything is sent, Resolvr shows you exactly which fields will be included. Those fields are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which forwards them to our AI provider, [AI PROVIDER, e.g. Anthropic], to generate the draft and return it to you. Our provider processes the text under its commercial terms, which [DO NOT PERMIT TRAINING ON IT — confirm].

Encrypted backup

Backup is off by default. When you turn it on, your record is encrypted on your device and only the encrypted file is uploaded. It is protected by a recovery phrase that only you hold. We can see that a backup exists, its size and when it was made; we cannot see what is inside. Deleting your account deletes your backups.

Analytics and cookies

This website uses Plausible Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without collecting personal data. The web app does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses essential storage on your device to hold your encrypted record and settings, and a session cookie if you sign in.
