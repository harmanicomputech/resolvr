How it works

Encryption on your device

When you set your PIN, Resolvr derives an encryption key from it with a slow key-derivation function and a random salt. Every incident and file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written to your browser's storage. The key is held only in memory while the app is unlocked and is discarded when it locks.

Tamper-evident timestamps

Each incident is hashed with SHA-256 together with the hash of the entry before it, forming a chain. Edits are saved as new versions rather than overwriting the original. Exports include a verification appendix so anyone can check the chain has not been altered. This shows whether a record changed after it was made; it cannot prove that what was written is true.

Backups

Backup is optional. When it is on, your data is encrypted on your device and only the ciphertext is uploaded. You keep a recovery phrase; we never see it, and we cannot reset it for you.

AI drafting

Drafting a document sends only the incident fields you select, shown to you before anything is sent, to our server, which passes them to our AI provider to write the draft. Drafting is optional and never happens in the background.

Limits you should know

  • If someone knows your PIN and has your unlocked device, they can see your record. Choose a PIN others can't guess.
  • Work devices and networks can be monitored by your employer. Use your own phone and connection where you can.
  • If you forget your PIN and have no backup, your record cannot be recovered. That is the cost of us not holding a key.
  • Stealth mode disguises the app. It does not hide it from someone who inspects your device closely.
