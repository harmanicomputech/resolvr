The short version

  • The incidents, notes, people, files and check-ins you add are encrypted on your device. We never receive them unencrypted, and we cannot read them.
  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, and we hold your email address only if you join our waitlist.
  • Some features send encrypted data to our server only when you use them: backup and sharing with a lawyer. Reminders store only a push address and a time.
  • AI drafting is optional. Only the details you choose are sent, to create the draft you asked for.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, and we never sell or rent data.
  • Resolvr helps you keep an organised record. It is not legal advice.

Who we are

Resolvr (“Resolvr”, “we”, “us”) is operated as a DBA of a Texas limited liability company. We are responsible for the limited personal data described here. Contact: resolvrapp@resolvrapp.com.

Your record stays on your device

The Android app shows the Resolvr web app inside a secure in-app browser (a WebView). What you add (incidents, people, notes, impact statements, wellbeing check-ins and evidence files) is stored in that app's private storage on your device.

Before anything is stored, it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a random key. That key is itself locked with your passcode (using Argon2id) and, if you add them, your passkey or recovery phrase. While the app is unlocked the key is kept in memory only, and it is forgotten when the app locks. We never receive your passcode, your recovery phrase, your key or the unencrypted contents of your record.

Because we cannot read your record, we cannot recover it if you forget your passcode and have no recovery phrase, and we cannot hand its contents to anyone, including your employer.

Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can refuse. Everything you capture is encrypted on your device.

  • Camera: to photograph evidence, such as a document or a notice.
  • Microphone: to record a voice note for an incident.
  • Photos and files: to add a screenshot or document you choose.
  • Notifications: for reminders, if you turn them on.
  • Biometrics (passkey): to unlock with your fingerprint or face, if you set it up. The biometric check happens on your device; we never receive it.

AI drafting

Drafting a letter or other document with AI is optional and happens only when you ask. You see and choose what is included first. Those details go to our server, which forwards them to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write the draft and send it back. We do not store the request or the draft, and we do not log its content. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on this content. Drafts are saved only on your device, encrypted.

Encrypted backup

Backup is off until you set it up. When you back up, your record is encrypted on your device first, and only that encrypted file is uploaded. It can be opened only with your 12-word recovery phrase, which only you hold. We can see that a backup exists, its size and when it changed, but not what is inside. Backups are identified by a code derived from your phrase, not by your name or email. You can delete the online copy at any time in Settings.

Sharing with a lawyer

If you create a share link, the incidents and files you choose are encrypted on your device with a new random key before upload. The key is only in the link itself, after the # sign, which browsers never send to a server, so we cannot open the copy. Links expire after 24 hours or 7 days, and you can turn one off at any time, which deletes the copy at once. Each time a link is opened we record the time and a general browser description (not the IP address), so you can see when it was used.

Reminders

Reminders are off unless you turn them on. Then your device gives us a push address, which we store with the time and days you chose. Reminders carry no text from us: your device decides what the notification says, and can disguise it. Your device's push service (for example Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) delivers it and can see that a reminder was sent, but not your record. Turning reminders off deletes the push address.

Analytics, ads and cookies

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Our website resolvrapp.com uses Plausible Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without personal data. The app uses only essential storage: your encrypted record and settings, and a security cookie that protects forms from forgery.

Who we share with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We use a small number of service providers, only to run Resolvr:

  • Our web hosting provider, which stores our servers, encrypted backups and share copies.
  • Our email service, to send waitlist emails.
  • Anthropic, only when you ask for an AI draft.
  • Your device's push service, only if you turn on reminders.
  • Plausible Analytics, for anonymous page counts on our website only.

We will disclose the limited data we hold if the law requires it. Because your record is end-to-end encrypted, we are not able to disclose its contents.

How long we keep data

  • Waitlist emails: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Encrypted backups: until you remove the online copy in the app.
  • Share links: the encrypted copy until the link expires or you turn it off; the link's record and view times are deleted 30 days after that.
  • Reminders: until you turn them off, or your device withdraws the push address.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.

Deleting your data

There is no account to close. You are in control of everything:

  • Everything on your device: in the app, open Settings › Your data › Delete everything. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, also erases it.
  • Your online backup: Settings › Backup › Remove the online copy (or tick “Also delete my encrypted online backup” when deleting everything).
  • Share links: Documents › Share with a lawyer › Turn off.
  • Reminders: Settings › Reminders › turn off.
  • Waitlist email: use the unsubscribe link in any email.

If you no longer have the app, or want us to delete anything else, email resolvrapp@resolvrapp.com and we will do so within 30 days. Backups are not linked to your name or email, so we cannot find one for you: please remove it in the app. A backup whose recovery phrase is lost can never be opened by anyone, including us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, Nigeria's NDPA, Canada's PIPEDA or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. You can export or delete everything on your device from the app's settings at any time.

Security

Data is encrypted on your device and sent only over HTTPS. The app can lock automatically, lock when you switch away, open as a calculator, and use a decoy passcode. Our server stores only encrypted files, outside any public folder, and limits how often requests can be made. No system is perfectly secure: a short passcode can be guessed by someone who copies your device's storage, so we recommend 6 digits or a passphrase. If you are worried your device is monitored, use your own phone and connection.

Children

Resolvr is for people at work and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
